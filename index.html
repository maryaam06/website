01 / story

why I started

Matcha started as just the thing I made for myself every day, the one part of a busy schedule where I slowed down and did something carefully with my hands. Sift the powder, heat the water to just the right temperature, whisk until it turns soft and foamy.

The more I made it, the more curious I got. I started experimenting: maple instead of sugar, a little lavender, fresh strawberry at the bottom of the glass, a cinnamon cold foam I kept adjusting until it finally tasted right. Every batch taught me something, and plenty of them went straight down the sink.

Before long I was making it for friends and family too.

Watching someone take their first sip of something I made from scratch is still my favourite part.

Making matcha taught me more than I expected: patience, paying attention to the small details, and not giving up when a recipe doesn't work the first time.

what it taught me

patience

Good matcha can't be rushed, and neither can getting a recipe right.

detail

Water temperature, ratios and timing all change how a cup tastes.

experimenting

Trying new flavours, keeping what works and learning from what doesn't.

feedback

Asking the people I make it for what they think, then making it better.

02 / method

how I make it

sift

Matcha clumps easily, so it always goes through a small strainer first. No lumps, ever.

heat

Water around 75 to 80°C, never boiling. Boiling water burns it and makes it bitter.

whisk

Fast, in a zig-zag "M" shape with a bamboo whisk until the top turns light and foamy.

pour

Sweetener first, then milk and ice, then the matcha poured slowly on top.

my recipes

everyday iced matcha

where it all started

You need

  • 1 tsp matcha
  • 60 ml warm water
  • 180 ml milk of choice
  • Ice, sweetener to taste

Make it

  1. Sift matcha, add water, whisk until foamy.
  2. Fill a glass with ice, milk and sweetener.
  3. Pour the matcha over the top.

strawberry matcha

my favourite

You need

  • 3 to 4 strawberries
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Everyday matcha base

Make it

  1. Mash strawberries with sugar in the glass.
  2. Add ice and milk.
  3. Top with whisked matcha and stir before drinking.

lavender matcha

soft and floral

Lavender syrup

  • 1/2 cup water + 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp dried culinary lavender

Make it

  1. Simmer water and sugar until dissolved.
  2. Add lavender, steep 15 min, strain.
  3. Use 1 tbsp syrup in the everyday matcha.

cinnamon cold foam

took me the longest to get right

You need

  • 3 tbsp heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp maple or vanilla syrup
  • A pinch of cinnamon

Make it

  1. Froth everything for about 20 seconds.
  2. Spoon over any iced matcha.
